# Lectern

Learn for the joy of it.

Somewhere along the way, learning turned into something you do for a grade, a degree or a job. It did not start that way. It started with wondering how something works.

Lectern is for that kind of learning. The world's universities have recorded and shared some of the finest teaching there is. We gathered it in one place and made it easy to follow: each course in order, each lecture with chapters and a searchable transcript made by AI.

There is no deadline and no exam. Watch a lecture over breakfast, or a chapter on the train. Stop when life gets busy and find your place when you come back.

Lectern is free to watch and shows no ads of its own; videos play through YouTube's player. It is independent, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the universities whose lectures it lists.

If you are curious, you belong here. [lectern.tv](https://lectern.tv)
